Bi‑Weekly Period Planner

Groups cashflow into 14-day periods from your income dates. Debit spend, bills, and card payoffs are actual cash out; credit card purchases are shown as informational (billed at payoff). Recurring incomes and recurring bills both auto-project across the horizon.

Period Controls

Periods are built as 14-day blocks anchored from the earliest income date (or today if none). Recurring bills and recurring incomes are auto-projected across this horizon (stopping at their end date, if one is set). Carry-forward rolls to the next period, then into subsequent months.
