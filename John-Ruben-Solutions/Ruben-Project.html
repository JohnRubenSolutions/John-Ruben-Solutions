Bi‑Weekly Period Planner

Groups cashflow into 14-day periods from your income dates. Debit spend, bills, and card payoffs are actual cash out; credit card purchases are shown as informational (billed at payoff). Recurring incomes and recurring bills both auto-project across the horizon.

Period Controls

The Projected Balance column and every line's "Balance after" follow this choice. Credit card spending is never subtracted — only card payoffs and bank-paid items — so nothing is counted twice.

Periods are built as 14-day blocks anchored from the earliest income date (or today if none). The planner always looks one year out from today. Recurring bills and recurring incomes are auto-projected across that year (stopping at their end date, if one is set). Carry-forward rolls to the next period, then into subsequent months.

Periods
Credit card purchases (and bills assigned to a credit card) add to that card's liability but don't leave your bank until you log a Payoff. Use "Details" on any period to see its individual transactions and change their dates — changes sync to Data Entry instantly.
