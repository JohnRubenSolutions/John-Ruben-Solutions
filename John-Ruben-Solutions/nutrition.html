New to cooking? Read this first
  • Low / medium / high heat: low = gentle bubbles, medium = steady sizzle, high = loud sizzle. If garlic smells sharp or turns dark brown, lower the heat.
  • Mince = chop into tiny pieces. Sauté = cook in a little oil, stirring often. Simmer = small bubbles, lid on or off as stated.
  • Chicken and fish are done when no pink remains inside (chicken 165°F / 74°C, fish 145°F / 63°C).
  • Use 1 tablespoon of oil or less per serving. Olive, mustard or canola oil are good choices. Avoid ghee, vanaspati and deep frying.
  • Keep salt low. Finish with lemon, lime or fresh herbs for extra flavour instead.
About the evidence. No trial can double-blind a meal, so food studies are randomized but not blinded. The patterns used here come from randomized trials: the Lyon Diet Heart Study (fewer cardiac deaths), PREDIMED (fewer heart events with olive oil or nuts), the Indo-Mediterranean Diet trial in Indian patients, and DASH/OmniHeart (lower blood pressure and cholesterol). No single recipe has been proven to cut mortality. Nutrition numbers are estimates. This is education, not medical advice: talk to your doctor if you have heart disease, diabetes, kidney disease or take blood thinners.
